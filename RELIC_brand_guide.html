RELIC

A custom-drawn serif wordmark with high-contrast, Didone-influenced capitals, wide tracking and flat bracketless serifs: antique character, contemporary restraint. The secondary mark is a glass chimney drawn in a single line, holding one small point — a preserved ember, a kept memory. No flame icon, no ornament, no gradients.

Wordmark

Use the ivory file on dark grounds (espresso, night, bronze, photography) and the espresso file on light grounds.

Horizontal lockup & compact mark

The compact monogram is for favicons, avatars and small footers. Its stroke is 3 units on a 64×100 grid; do not use it below 20 px tall, and use the full wordmark wherever the name needs to be read.

Typography

A New Form of the Flame

Headlines — Cormorant Garamond (Light 300, Regular, Italic). Large, light, tightly leaded; italic for emphasis. SIL Open Font License.
Body & labels — Inter (Regular 400, Medium 500). Labels set in capitals at +0.22em tracking. SIL Open Font License.
Both are self-hosted via @fontsource; no third-party font requests.

Usage

  • Clear space around the wordmark: at least the height of the letter “I” (cap height) on every side.
  • Minimum wordmark width: 80 px on screen, 20 mm in print.
  • Only the supplied colours: ivory #F2EAE0 or espresso #30271F. Never brass, amber or gradients on the wordmark.
  • Do not stretch, outline, add shadows or glows, rotate, or retypeset the name in a font.
  • Place over photography only where the area behind it is calm and dark enough for ivory to hold strong contrast.
  • Never place a flame icon or ornament beside the mark.
